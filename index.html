Overview

A NAAC A+ University in the Heart of Delhi NCR

Shree Guru Gobind Singh Tricentenary University (SGT University) is a private university in Gurugram, established in 2013 and recognised by UGC with 12(B) status. Its 100-acre campus brings medicine, engineering, management, law, design and more together in one place.

FAQs

SGT University Admission FAQs

Is SGT University approved by UGC?

Yes. SGT University is recognised under Section 2(f) of the UGC Act, 1956, and holds UGC 12(B) status. It is also a member of the Association of Indian Universities (AIU).

Is SGT University NAAC accredited?

Yes. SGT University was accredited with an A+ grade by NAAC in 2023, making it one of India's youngest institutions with an A+ grade. It also received ICAR accreditation in 2024.

Which courses does SGT University offer?

More than 200 programme options across 19 faculties, at diploma, undergraduate, postgraduate and PhD level. These include MBBS, BDS, B.Sc Nursing, BPT, B.Pharm, BAMS, B.Tech, BCA, BBA, B.Com, BA LLB, B.Des, BHM, BA (Hons) programmes, B.Ed, MBA, MCA and many more.

What is the placement record at SGT University?

In 2026, the university reported a near 90% annual placement rate, 275+ campus recruiters and a highest package of ₹42 LPA. These are university-wide figures across all programmes.

What is the hostel fee at SGT University?

On-campus hostel fees are ₹2,00,000 a year for twin sharing, ₹1,80,000 for three sharing and ₹1,40,000 for four sharing. Air-conditioned rooms cost extra. Boys and girls have separate hostels. Your counsellor will confirm current fees.

Are scholarships available?

Yes. SGT University offers merit-based scholarships and financial aid for eligible students. Register on this page and a counsellor will tell you what you may qualify for.

Does SGT University have a hospital on campus?

Yes. The 810+ bed SGT Hospital is on campus, along with medical and dental clinics, and supports clinical training for health sciences students.

Is transport available from Delhi NCR?

Yes. The university runs 66+ buses with bus and shuttle services across Delhi-NCR.

Where is SGT University located?

Gurgaon-Badli Road, Chandu, Budhera, Gurugram, Haryana 122505, in Delhi NCR. The campus is connected by metro, road and air, and is near IGI Airport.
